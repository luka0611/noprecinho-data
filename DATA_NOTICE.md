# Data notice

- **Source:** public prices from electronic receipts (NFC-e/NF-e) published by the state service **Preço da Hora Bahia**
  (SEFAZ-BA, Portaria nº 42/2020). This project is independent and has no link to the Government of Bahia or SEFAZ-BA.
- **Not offers:** as stated in art. 5 of Portaria 42/2020, these prices do not represent an offer by the merchant
  (CDC arts. 30–35). Prices may change at the checkout.
  *Preços de notas fiscais recentes. Não são ofertas e podem mudar no caixa.*
- **Privacy:** no buyer data is collected. Stores that are one-person businesses without a trade name are excluded;
  legal names of individual entrepreneurs and store phone numbers are never republished.
- **Corrections / objections:** stores can ask for corrections or removal via the contact on the project's `/bot` page
  (answered within 7 days).
- Other sources, when used: Receita Federal open data (via minhareceita.org), Open Food Facts images (CC BY-SA).
